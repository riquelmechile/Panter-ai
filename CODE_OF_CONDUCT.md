# Code of conduct

Panter-ai follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith, in English, in Spanish or in any other language.
- Criticise code, designs and decisions, never people.
- Give evidence: a failing test, a log, a commit. That is how this project settles disagreements.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change that does not fit the project.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Publishing someone's private information, credentials or deployment details without permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive posts, including spam generated by AI tools.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and every other project space, and to anyone acting on behalf of the project elsewhere. Automated agents that post here count as the person who runs them.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the repository owner, through the contact listed on their GitHub profile. Do not use a public issue. Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code. The response depends on how serious the problem is and whether it repeats.
